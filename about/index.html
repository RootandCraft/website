My Story

Rooted in creativity

Hi, I'm Cass, the founder and creative heart behind Root & Craft.


I've been an artist and maker for as long as I can remember. As a young girl, I spent countless hours drawing horses, and as I grew older, my curiosity led me to explore sculpting, photography, and other creative pursuits.


I grew up on a fruit farm, where I spent much of my time outdoors, surrounded by nature and the changing seasons. I loved taking long walks with my camera, observing the little details that might otherwise go unnoticed and finding inspiration in the world around me.


I believe art has taught me to see the world a little differently, to notice the beauty in textures, colours, shapes, and the smallest details. That curiosity and appreciation for the natural world eventually led me to a career in interior design, where I could bring my creativity to life through beautiful materials, thoughtful details, and the spaces we create around us.


My love of nature, art, and the simple joy of making something by hand inspired me to start Root & Craft.


Today, I bring those passions together through thoughtfully designed experiences that encourage self-expression, meaningful connection, and wellbeing.

Cass

Our Approach

The intention behind every detail

Root & Craft is guided by a simple belief: that thoughtful, creative experiences and handmade gifts can bring people together, support wellbeing, and leave a lighter footprint.


From the first idea to the final detail, we take time to thoughtfully design each workshop, activation, and gift — choosing natural, high-quality materials, working with small and local suppliers where possible, and using recyclable or reusable packaging.
