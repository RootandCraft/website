Meet the Founder

Rooted in making

Root & Craft was founded on a simple belief: creating with your hands is one of the most grounding and restorative experiences available to us, and it becomes even more meaningful when shared with others.


I'm Casandra, the founder of Root & Craft. I design and host hands-on creative experiences for private groups, corporate teams, and special events, and make handmade gifts for the occasions that call for them.


Every booking starts with a conversation. I'll learn about your group, your event, and how you'd like people to feel, then shape the experience around it, from the project and materials to the finishing details.
