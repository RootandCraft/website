My Story

Rooted in creativity

Hi, I'm Cass, the founder and creative heart behind Root & Craft.


I've been an artist and maker for as long as I can remember. Creativity has always been a natural part of my life, from exploring different art forms to building a career in interior design, where I developed a deep appreciation for beautiful materials, thoughtful details, and the spaces we create around us.


Over the years, I've come to appreciate how rewarding it is to make something with your own hands, especially when that experience is shared with others. That love of creativity, connection, and wellbeing is what inspired me to create Root & Craft.


Today, I bring those passions together through experiences that encourage people to explore their creativity, connect with one another, and enjoy the simple pleasure of making.

Cass
