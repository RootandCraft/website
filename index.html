Welcome to Root & Craft

Creative experiences, lasting impressions.

Root & Craft brings creativity and wellness to corporate teams, private gatherings, and special events through guided creative workshops, thoughtfully designed wellness activations, and customized handmade gifting.


Inspired by nature and the joy of creating, our experiences are designed to bring people together, encourage connection, and make every gathering a little more memorable.


As a mobile creative studio, we bring everything to your office, venue, or home throughout Metro Vancouver. We take care of the details so you can simply gather and enjoy the experience.
