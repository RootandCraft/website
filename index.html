Welcome to Root & Craft

Hands-on experiences, made for gathering

Root & Craft brings creativity and wellness to private groups, corporate teams, and special events. We host guided clay workshops, design sensory activations that fit naturally into your event, and make handmade gifts in quantity for your guests, clients, or team.


It all comes back to a simple idea: making something by hand is grounding, and it means more when it's shared. Every experience is beginner-friendly, professionally facilitated, and shaped around the people in the room.

The Root & Craft Approach

Made by hand, shaped around you

We keep things warm, unhurried, and easy to join. Nobody needs to arrive feeling creative, just curious. We take care of the details so you and your guests can simply be present.

  • Designed around your group, occasion, and setting
  • Tactile, nature-inspired materials: clay, beeswax, botanicals, and essential oils
  • All materials and tools provided
  • Beginner-friendly, with guidance at every step
  • Professionally facilitated
