Welcome to Root & Craft

Creative experiences, lasting impressions.

Root & Craft brings creativity and wellness to corporate teams, private gatherings, and special events through guided creative workshops, thoughtfully designed wellness activations, and customized handmade gifting.


Inspired by nature and the joy of creating, our experiences are designed to bring people together, encourage connection, and make every gathering a little more memorable.


We're a mobile studio, so we bring everything to your office, venue, or home across Metro Vancouver and the North Shore. You simply gather.

The Root & Craft Approach

Made by hand, shaped around you

We keep things warm, unhurried, and easy to join. Nobody needs to arrive feeling creative, just curious. We take care of the details so you and your guests can simply be present.

  • Designed around your group, occasion, and setting
  • We come to you, with everything set up and cleared away
  • Tactile, nature-inspired materials: clay, beeswax, botanicals, and essential oils
  • All materials and tools provided
  • Beginner-friendly, with guidance at every step
  • Professionally facilitated
