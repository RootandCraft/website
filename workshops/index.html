Creative Workshops

Make something by hand, together

Immersive, hands-on creative experiences that bring people together through making, exploration, and connection.

We come to youIn person or virtualPrivate & corporate groupsNo experience needed

We bring creativity to you with guided, hands-on workshops for corporate teams and private gatherings. Using carefully selected, high-quality materials, guests explore new techniques, create something by hand, and connect through a welcoming experience suited to all skill levels.

In Person

In-Person Creative Workshops

Our two signature categories, Air-Dry Clay Creations and Polymer Clay Jewellery, with project options tailored to your group. We come to your office, venue, or home and take care of all setup and cleanup. In-person workshops generally run 1.5–3 hours.

Clay

Air-Dry Clay Creations

Explore clay techniques to create beautiful, functional, and decorative pieces. A relaxed, hands-on experience where everyone leaves with something uniquely their own.

Example projects

  • Trinket dishes
  • Ring organizers
  • Candle holders
  • Incense holders
Jewellery

Polymer Clay Jewellery

A more detailed workshop, especially well suited to smaller corporate teams and private groups. Participants explore polymer clay techniques, colours, shapes, and finishing details to create their own handcrafted accessories.

Example projects

  • Earrings
  • Pendants
  • Cufflinks
Virtual Kits

Remote & Hybrid Teams

Virtual Corporate Workshops

Bring your team together through a shared creative experience, no matter where everyone is located.


Our virtual workshops are designed for remote and hybrid teams, offering a welcoming opportunity to connect, explore creativity, and make something together from the comfort of home.


Each participant receives a thoughtfully prepared creative kit with the materials needed for their project, followed by live, guided instruction.


From air-dry clay creations to seasonal and nature-inspired projects, we'll work with you to select an experience that suits your team, occasion, and budget.
